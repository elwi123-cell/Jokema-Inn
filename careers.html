Jokema Inn is listed at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. Public business listings associate the venue with an inn, café, bar and car-wash service, while local directory material also refers to live music and social activities.

WHAT WE VALUE

Skills and qualities that support good service.

Hospitality and service work depends on consistent attention to customers, colleagues and the workplace. Applicants making an enquiry should be ready to explain their relevant experience, practical skills and availability.

Depending on the position, useful experience may include hospitality, food preparation, customer service, housekeeping, cleaning, vehicle detailing, general maintenance, cash handling or office administration. Management will confirm which qualifications, licences or experience are required for a specific role.

Applicants should be prepared to follow workplace instructions, safety procedures, lawful service requirements and reasonable standards of conduct. Do not assume that a qualification or experience requirement applies to every role; check the details of the actual vacancy.

HOW TO ENQUIRE

A straightforward application process.

Jokema Inn's publicly listed telephone contact is the best verified starting point for a career enquiry. Call management to ask whether a vacancy is available and obtain the approved application instructions before sending documents or visiting the premises.

  1. Ask about current recruitment. Confirm whether the business is accepting applications and which position, if any, is available.
  2. Request the role details. Ask about duties, work location, hours, required experience, qualifications and closing date.
  3. Confirm how to apply. Obtain the correct submission method and the name or title of the person receiving applications.
  4. Prepare relevant documents. Follow the instructions provided by management and submit only the information requested.
  5. Keep a record. Note the date of your enquiry and the application method. Follow up politely if management provides a timeframe.
