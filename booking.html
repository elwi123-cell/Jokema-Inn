How to make a booking enquiry

Use the contact details on this page to ask about your intended dates and requirements. An enquiry is not a confirmed reservation. Your booking should only be treated as confirmed after the inn has directly accepted it and communicated the applicable arrangements.

02

Prepare Your Enquiry

Information to have ready

Sharing these details will help the team understand your requirements and respond more clearly.

Your booking enquiry checklist

  • Your preferred arrival and departure dates
  • The number of guests, including adults and children
  • Your preferred room type or accommodation requirements
  • Any accessibility, family or other special requirements
  • Your contact name and a reliable phone number
  • Any questions about payment or booking conditions

Before you confirm

Ask management to clarify the total cost, what is included, check-in and check-out arrangements, any deposit or payment requirements, and the applicable cancellation conditions.
