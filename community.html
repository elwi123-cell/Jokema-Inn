Our local setting

Hospitality begins with connection

Jokema Inn is located in Butibam, Lae, Morobe Province. The Inn is part of a wider community shaped by local people, businesses, traditions and everyday life.

We believe visitors and local communities benefit when hospitality is approached with courtesy, consideration and respect. This page shares general community values and visitor guidance; it does not claim specific sponsorships, community projects or formal partnerships.

Find Jokema Inn
Community-minded hospitality

Principles that guide positive connections

Every guest, visitor and organisation can contribute to a welcoming environment through small, considerate actions.

01

Respect Local Culture

Recognise local customs, listen respectfully and seek guidance when you are unsure about cultural expectations.

02

Support Local Enterprise

Where appropriate, consider local businesses and service providers and engage with them fairly and respectfully.

03

Care for the Environment

Dispose of waste responsibly, conserve resources and help keep shared spaces clean and welcoming.

04

Be a Considerate Visitor

Respect neighbours, avoid unnecessary disturbance and follow reasonable directions in public and private spaces.

05

Build Positive Relationships

Communicate courteously with residents, workers, other guests and community representatives.

06

Share Constructive Feedback

Raise questions or concerns through appropriate channels so they can be understood and addressed.

For visitors and guests

Simple ways to show community respect

When spending time in Butibam or elsewhere in Lae, keep these practical considerations in mind.

Ask Before Taking Photos

Seek permission before photographing people, homes, private property or community activities.

Respect Shared Spaces

Keep noise considerate, avoid littering and respect the use of roads, walkways and public facilities.

Follow Local Advice

Check current local guidance before visiting unfamiliar places or attending community events.

Community information: Any community programs, donations, sponsorships, partnerships or public events involving Jokema Inn should be confirmed through official announcements or directly with management. No particular initiative or partnership is represented as currently active on this page.
