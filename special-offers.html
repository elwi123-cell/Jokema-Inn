OFFERS & ENQUIRIES

Find out what is available for your visit.

Jokema Inn is a hospitality and service venue in Butibam, Lae, associated in public business listings with accommodation, café and dining, bar service, live music and car-wash enquiries. Visitors can contact management to ask about current rates, packages or promotional arrangements across the services they are interested in.

Promotions can be introduced, changed or withdrawn by the business. For that reason, this page does not publish invented discounts, package prices, expiry dates or eligibility conditions. The team should confirm every offer directly before a guest makes a booking or purchase decision.

When making an enquiry, mention the service you need, your preferred date and the number of people in your group. This will help management advise whether any relevant arrangement is available.
