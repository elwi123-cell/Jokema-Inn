Plan Your Event

Information to prepare before contacting us.

A few details will help management understand your requirements and advise whether your proposed event can be accommodated.

  • Type and purpose of the event
  • Preferred date and expected start and finish times
  • Estimated number of guests
  • Seating, space and accessibility requirements
  • Food, refreshments or catering expectations
  • Audio, music, decoration or equipment needs
  • Your preferred contact details
