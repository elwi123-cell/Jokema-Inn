Travel information

Arriving at Jokema Inn

Jokema Inn is located at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. Use the full address when arranging a taxi, private vehicle or other transport to the property.

If you are unfamiliar with Lae or travelling from outside the city, plan your route in advance and allow extra time for traffic, weather and changing road conditions.

Ways to plan your journey

The following are general transport modes visitors may consider. They are not confirmation that Jokema Inn operates, provides or endorses any particular transport service.

01

Private Vehicle

Use the property address for navigation and confirm parking arrangements with management before arrival.

02

Taxi or Hired Vehicle

Arrange a vehicle through a provider you trust. Agree on the destination and fare directly with the transport provider.

03

Public Transport

Check current routes, operating arrangements and the safest convenient drop-off point with reliable local sources.

04

Airport Connections

If arriving by air, plan onward transport from Lae's airport and confirm journey details before departure.

05

Visitors travelling from other parts of Papua New Guinea should confirm schedules, road conditions and connections with operators.

06

Local Excursions

For visits around Lae, confirm destination access, return transport and current local advice before setting out.
