A MULTI-SERVICE VENUE

One venue, several listed services.

Public business listings identify Jokema’s Inn at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, and describe the business as “Cafe | Bar | Car wash”. A separate accommodation directory lists Jokema’s Inn under hotels in Morobe. PNG Haus Bung also described it as a mini hotel in Butibam village in a September 2025 report.

These sources support presenting Jokema’s Inn as a venue associated publicly with accommodation, café, bar and car-wash services. They do not provide a complete facilities inventory, floor plan, equipment list, accessibility audit or current service menu.

This page separates services identified in public listings from details that still require confirmation. It does not claim that every service is operating at all times or that any unverified amenity is available to guests.
