Changes & Cancellations

Contact the inn as early as possible.

If your travel plans change, contact Jokema Inn directly. The exact cancellation deadlines, fees, deposit treatment and refund arrangements for a reservation must be confirmed by management.

01

How to request a cancellation

Contact Jokema Inn using the telephone number below. Clearly provide the name used for the reservation, the booking dates and your contact details so management can identify the enquiry or reservation.

02

Changes to a reservation

If you need to change your arrival date, departure date, guest numbers or other booking details, contact management as early as possible. Changes are subject to confirmation and availability.

03

Deposits and payments

Ask management to explain whether any deposit or advance payment applies and how it is treated if a booking is changed or cancelled. Do not assume that a deposit is refundable or non-refundable unless the applicable condition has been confirmed.

04

Refunds and applicable charges

Any refund eligibility, processing arrangements, cancellation charge or other fee must be confirmed directly with Jokema Inn. Request an explanation of the conditions before accepting or making a payment.

05

Late changes and non-arrival

If you expect to arrive later than planned or cannot attend, notify management promptly. Ask how late arrival, failure to arrive or a shortened stay is handled under the conditions of your reservation.

06

Keep a record

Retain the booking confirmation and any messages or payment records. When management confirms a cancellation or change, keep that confirmation for your own records.

Important: This page is general guest guidance, not a statement of fixed cancellation deadlines, refund entitlements or fees. Jokema Inn management must confirm and publish its official cancellation rules. Always rely on the specific terms communicated and accepted for your reservation.
Before You Make a Booking

Ask these questions in advance.

To avoid uncertainty, clarify the cancellation conditions before confirming your stay or paying a deposit.

  • What is the deadline for cancelling without a charge, if one applies?
  • Are deposits or advance payments refundable, transferable or retained?
  • Are there different conditions for late cancellations or non-arrival?
  • How should a cancellation or change request be submitted?
  • How will management confirm that the booking has been changed or cancelled?
