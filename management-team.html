BUSINESS LEADERSHIP

Publicly identified operator.

A news report published by PNG Haus Bung on 8 September 2025 identifies popular PNG musician and entrepreneur Honlly Isaac, widely known by his stage name Jokema, as the operator of Jokema Inn, described in the article as a mini hotel in Butibam village, Lae.

The report says that for a three-day Pre-Independence Celebration at Erap Primary School, Jokema donated his professional sound system, assigned a young stage and technical crew from the hotel to manage production, and provided cash support for refreshments and logistics.

This public report is the clearest source located for an individual associated with the operation of the Inn. It does not publish a formal organisation chart, a general manager's name, department heads, or a full staff list. This page therefore identifies the operator as reported and does not invent additional management names or titles.

TEAMWORK IN ACTION

A publicly reported example from 2025.

PNG Haus Bung reported that Jokema supported Erap Primary School’s three-day Pre-Independence Celebration in September 2025. As described in the article, the contribution included a professional sound system, a young stage and technical crew from the hotel to manage production, and cash assistance for refreshments and logistics.

This report provides a documented example of the operator and members of the hotel’s technical team working on an activity outside the venue. SERVICE STANDARDS

What guests should be able to expect.

A professional hospitality experience depends on respectful communication, accurate information and reliable service. The following principles are recommended standards for the Inn to confirm and adopt as its official guest-service commitments.

  • Courtesy and respect for every guest and visitor.
  • Clear communication about availability, rates and service conditions.
  • Clean and well-maintained guest and public areas.
  • Responsible handling of enquiries, feedback and complaints.
  • Attention to workplace safety, hygiene and responsible service.
  • Protection of guest privacy and personal information.

These are proposed service principles for management approval, not a statement that a formal Jokema Inn service charter has already been published.
