ROOM DIRECTORY

Confirm the room that suits your stay.

Jokema’s Inn is included in third-party accommodation listings for Morobe and is identified as a hotel business at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae. A 2025 report by PNG Haus Bung also describes Jokema Inn as a mini hotel in Butibam village.

However, the public sources reviewed do not provide an official list of room names, room counts, bed configurations, photographs, guest capacities or room prices. It would be misleading to present example categories as confirmed products offered by the property.

The room options on this page are therefore planning and enquiry categories only. Ask management which configurations are actually available on your dates, and request confirmation of the total price and included facilities before making travel arrangements.

ROOM REQUIREMENTS

Room arrangements to ask about.

Use these categories to describe your needs when you contact the Inn. They are not confirmation that Jokema Inn currently offers each room type.

ROOM ENQUIRY 01

Single guest

Ask whether a room for one person is available, the bed configuration, whether the bathroom is private or shared, and whether the quoted rate is per room or per person.

ROOM ENQUIRY 02

Two guests

For two people, confirm whether one shared bed or separate beds can be provided, the maximum occupancy and whether the price changes according to the number of guests.

ROOM ENQUIRY 03

Twin-bed arrangement

If guests need separate beds, ask whether a twin configuration exists or can be arranged, and whether bedding is included for both occupants.

ROOM ENQUIRY 04

Family accommodation

Explain the number and ages of children travelling. Ask about suitable capacity, extra bedding, adjoining rooms and any child-related charges or conditions.

ROOM ENQUIRY 05

Work or project stay

For work assignments, ask about multi-night or extended-stay arrangements, room servicing, laundry access, payment intervals and whether a written invoice is available.

ROOM ENQUIRY 06

Specific access needs

Discuss stairs, ground-floor access, mobility requirements, bathroom access and any other essential needs before confirming the booking.

ROOM RATES & AVAILABILITY

Request a current quote.

No official Jokema Inn room rate card or live room-availability calendar was located in the public sources reviewed. Rates from other Lae accommodation providers are not Jokema Inn rates and must not be treated as a guide to its prices.

When enquiring, give the exact dates, arrival time, number of guests and preferred room arrangement. Ask for the complete amount in Papua New Guinea kina (PGK), including any deposit, additional guest fee or other charge.

Before paying, confirm the official payment recipient and method directly with the Inn. Request a booking confirmation showing the dates, room arrangement, total price, amount paid and remaining balance. Keep your receipt.

BOOKING PREPARATION

Details to provide when requesting a room.

Arrival and departure

Provide check-in and check-out dates, expected arrival time and the number of nights required.

Guest numbers

State the number of adults and children, including children’s ages where relevant.

Bed preference

Explain whether you need one bed, separate beds, additional bedding or a family arrangement.

Essential amenities

List any must-have facilities such as a private bathroom, air conditioning, accessible entry or parking.

Payment questions

Ask about deposits, accepted payment methods, receipts, payment deadlines and cancellation conditions.

Special circumstances

Tell the Inn about late arrival, mobility needs, work-related requirements or other matters that could affect the stay.
