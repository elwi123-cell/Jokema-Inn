BUSINESS PROFILE

A local hospitality venue in Lae.

Jokema’s Inn is publicly listed at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. The business directory entry identifies it as “Jokema’s Inn | Cafe | Bar | Car wash” and categorises it within hotels and motels. Other Lae directory listings describe the venue as “Jokema’s Inn, Bar & Cafe” and place it in Butibam Village, Butibam Road.

These public listings establish the venue’s name, general location and the service categories associated with the business. They do not provide a verified company history, ownership profile, founding date, room inventory, official management biographies or a complete current service catalogue. This page therefore avoids presenting unverified background as fact.

The purpose of this website is to bring the available public information together in one place and provide a clear way for prospective guests and local visitors to contact the venue directly.
