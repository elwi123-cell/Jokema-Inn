Guest Information

Understand the arrangements before you confirm.

These are general booking-information guidelines to help guests know what to discuss with Jokema Inn management. Specific rates, payment requirements, check-in arrangements and other operational conditions must be confirmed directly with the inn.

On this page

1. Enquiries and reservations2. Availability and rates3. Guest information4. Payment and deposits5. Arrival and departure6. Guest responsibilities7. Changes and cancellations8. Confirmation and questions

1. Enquiries and reservations

Guests are encouraged to contact Jokema Inn directly to enquire about accommodation. An enquiry, telephone conversation or request for information does not by itself constitute a confirmed reservation.

A reservation should only be regarded as confirmed when management has expressly accepted the booking and communicated the relevant details to the guest.

2. Availability and rates

Room availability, room categories, rates and any applicable inclusions may vary. Guests should confirm the current information with Jokema Inn before making travel arrangements.

  • Confirm the dates and number of nights requested.
  • Ask which room options are available for the required dates.
  • Confirm the applicable rate, total amount and what is included.
  • Ask whether any additional charges or conditions apply.

3. Guest information

When making an enquiry or reservation, guests should provide accurate information, including their contact details, intended arrival and departure dates, number of guests and any relevant accommodation requirements. Management may request further details to administer a booking.

4. Payment and deposits

Any deposit, advance payment, accepted payment method, payment deadline or balance requirement must be confirmed directly with Jokema Inn management before payment is made.

Guests should request clear instructions and retain any payment record or confirmation provided. Do not assume that a particular payment method or deposit arrangement is available unless management has confirmed it.

5. Arrival and departure

Check-in and check-out times, early arrival, late departure and luggage arrangements should be discussed with management when arranging the stay. These details are not published as fixed terms on this page and must be confirmed for the relevant booking.

6. Guest responsibilities

Guests are expected to act respectfully towards staff, other guests and the property. Guests should follow any reasonable house rules or safety instructions communicated by management, take care of the premises and report any issue requiring attention.

Any specific rules relating to visitors, noise, smoking, property damage, children or use of facilities should be clarified with the inn before or during the stay.

7. Changes and cancellations

Requests to change dates, guest numbers or other reservation details should be made directly with Jokema Inn as early as possible. Whether a change can be accommodated depends on availability and the conditions confirmed by management.

Cancellation deadlines, refund eligibility, fees and any applicable exceptions must be confirmed directly. Please review the Cancellation Policy and verify the terms that apply to your reservation.

8. Confirmation and questions

Before travelling, guests should ensure they have received direct confirmation of the reservation, dates, room arrangements, total cost, payment instructions and arrival information.

If any part of the booking arrangement is unclear, contact Jokema Inn management for clarification before relying on it.

Important publication note: This page provides general guest guidance, not a complete or legally reviewed contract. Jokema Inn's management should confirm, approve and publish its official operational terms, rates, payment rules, check-in/check-out times and cancellation conditions. Where a specific condition has not been confirmed by management, do not assume that it applies.
