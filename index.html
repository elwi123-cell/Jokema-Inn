GET TO KNOW US

A hospitality stop in the Butibam area of Lae.

Jokema Inn is listed at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. Public business and social-directory listings associate the venue with an inn, café, bar and car-wash service. A Lae dining and entertainment listing also identifies Jokema’s Inn as a café and bar venue in Butibam Village.

Visitors may use this website as a starting point to explore the venue’s publicly described services, find contact information and make an enquiry. The website is designed to bring the different service areas together while making it clear which information needs direct confirmation from management.

Whether you are considering an overnight stay, meeting friends for a meal, asking about the bar or live entertainment, or checking car-wash arrangements, please contact the business first for the latest details.
