For organisations

Make your corporate accommodation enquiry

Jokema Inn welcomes enquiries from businesses, government offices, non-government organisations, project teams and other institutions seeking information about accommodation or potential arrangements in Lae.

If you are coordinating travel for employees, consultants, visiting personnel or a group, contact management to discuss your proposed dates and requirements. All arrangements are subject to confirmation by the Inn.

Start an Enquiry
Corporate enquiry areas

How we can discuss your requirements

Use the following categories to describe what your organisation is looking for. They are enquiry topics only and do not imply that specific corporate packages or services are currently available.

01

Business Accommodation

Ask about room options, requested dates, guest numbers, rates and any applicable booking conditions.

02

Project Team Visits

Discuss accommodation needs for project personnel, consultants or temporary teams working in the Lae area.

03

Group Reservations

Provide the expected number of guests and travel schedule so management can advise what may be accommodated.

04

Longer-Stay Enquiries

Share your anticipated duration and requirements for management to review possible arrangements.

05

Corporate Events

Enquire whether your proposed meeting or function requirements can be supported at the property.

06

Partnership Discussions

Organisations may contact management to introduce a proposal for consideration without assuming an existing partnership.

Prepare your request

Information to include in your enquiry

Providing clear details will help management understand your organisation's needs and respond with relevant information.

Organisation detailsCompany or institution name and the business contact person.
Travel datesProposed arrival and departure dates, including flexibility if any.
Guest numbersNumber of travellers and any room or occupancy preferences.
Purpose of visitBusiness travel, project deployment, training, meetings or another purpose.
Special requirementsAny relevant accessibility, scheduling or logistical considerations.
Billing preferencesAsk about invoicing, deposits, payment methods and account arrangements.
Important: Corporate rates, credit facilities, invoicing terms, long-stay discounts, group packages, meeting facilities and service levels are not confirmed by this page. They must be discussed and agreed directly with Jokema Inn management in writing before any commitment is made.
Business contact

Discuss a corporate requirement

Contact the management team with a concise outline of your request. For accommodation, include the dates and number of guests. For a partnership or commercial proposal, include your organisation details and the purpose of the discussion.
