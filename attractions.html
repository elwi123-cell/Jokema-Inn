Places to Explore

Experience the character of Lae.

From green city spaces to World War II heritage and local community life, Lae and the wider Morobe region offer a range of experiences. Confirm access, current conditions and any fees before travelling.

Lae National Botanic Gardens

A major green space with botanical collections, conservation and education activities. The gardens were established in 1949 and revived for public access in 2015.

Official gardens information ↗

Lae War Cemetery

A Commonwealth war cemetery commemorating service personnel who died during the New Guinea campaigns. Visitors are encouraged to approach the memorial respectfully.

Lae tourism information ↗

Unitech Rainforest Habitat

A nature and wildlife attraction associated with the Papua New Guinea University of Technology. Check visitor access and arrangements before setting out.

Lae tourism information ↗

Mount Lunaman Lookout

A city viewpoint associated with views over Lae and its harbour. Check local advice on safe access; do not enter closed or restricted tunnels.

Lae City Tourism Bureau ↗

Lae Main Market

Explore local produce, everyday commerce and the energy of city life. Visit during suitable daytime hours and follow local guidance for safety and respectful photography.

Lae visitor guide ↗

Morobe Showgrounds

The showgrounds are associated with the annual Morobe Show and major community, agricultural and cultural gatherings. Event dates and public access vary by programme.

Lae City Tourism Bureau ↗
