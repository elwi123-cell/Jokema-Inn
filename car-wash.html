JOKEMA INN CAR WASH

Vehicle-care enquiries in the Butibam area.

Jokema Inn is publicly listed at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. Its business listing associates the venue with accommodation, café and bar services, and car washing.

This page provides a direct starting point for people who want to enquire about the car-wash service. Before visiting, contact the team to establish whether the service is operating, what vehicle types can be accommodated and what arrangements apply.
