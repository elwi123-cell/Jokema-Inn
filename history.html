A RESPONSIBLE ACCOUNT OF OUR BACKGROUND

What can be confirmed about Jokema Inn?

Public sources identify Jokema’s Inn as a hospitality business in Butibam Village, Lae, Morobe Province. A September 2025 report by PNG Haus Bung describes musician and entrepreneur Honlly Isaac, known by the stage name Jokema, as operating Jokema Inn, a mini hotel located in Butibam village, Lae. The article also reports on his support for a Pre-Independence Celebration at Erap Primary School in 2025.

A separate business directory lists “Jokema’s Inn | Cafe | Bar | Car wash” at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, and provides the telephone number 7052 3210. Other online listings identify the venue as Jokema’s Inn, Bar & Cafe.

These sources provide a useful public snapshot of the business and its association with its operator. However, they do not state the year Jokema Inn was founded, the date it first opened to guests, its original business structure, or a complete timeline of its development. This page does not invent those details.

THE PLACE WHERE THE INN OPERATES

Butibam and the wider Lae setting.

Jokema Inn is located in Butibam, a village on the outskirts of Lae in Morobe Province. Public mapping data places the mapped Jokema’s Inn feature in the Butibam area, near Hengali Village and Chinatown. The mapped point is useful for identifying the general location, but it should not be treated as a substitute for confirming the exact entrance or directions with the venue.

Butibam has its own long-standing community and land history. A 2010 report in The National records a long-running land matter involving clans from Butibam and Kamkumung, while historical writing about the Lae area also discusses Butibam and neighbouring communities. These sources provide context about the locality; they do not establish the ownership, history or founding of Jokema Inn itself.

Keeping the distinction clear matters: the history of a village and the history of an individual business are related to place, but they are not the same record.

COMMUNITY & PUBLIC ACTIVITY

A reported example of community support.

In a report published on 8 September 2025, PNG Haus Bung stated that Jokema personally sponsored a three-day Pre-Independence Celebration for Erap Primary School in the Wampar LLG of Huon Gulf District, Morobe Province. The report says he contributed a professional sound system, assigned a young stage and technical crew from the hotel to support production, and provided cash assistance for refreshments and logistics.

This is a specific reported activity, not proof of a permanent community programme or an ongoing sponsorship policy. If Jokema Inn has an established community engagement programme, management can provide its official objectives, examples and contact details for publication on the Community page.
