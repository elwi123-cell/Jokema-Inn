ABOUT THE STAY

A listed accommodation venue in Butibam.

Jokema’s Inn appears in public accommodation directories for Morobe and is classified by a business directory under hotels and motels. The listing identifies the property at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea, and gives the contact telephone number 7052 3210.

A 2025 news report by PNG Haus Bung describes Jokema Inn as a mini hotel located in Butibam village, Lae. This provides public support for describing the venue as an accommodation business. However, the public sources reviewed do not provide a verified room inventory, room descriptions, guest capacity, nightly prices, check-in or check-out times, or a current availability calendar.

For that reason, this page does not advertise unverified room types or prices. Guests should contact the Inn directly to confirm what is currently offered and whether a room is available for their intended dates.

BEFORE YOU RESERVE

Find the right room for your visit.

The room categories below are enquiry prompts to help guests explain what they need. They are not confirmed room types currently available at Jokema Inn. Ask management which options exist at the property and what each includes.

ENQUIRY TYPE 01

Single occupancy

Ask whether a room suitable for one guest is available, what bedding it provides, whether the bathroom is private or shared, and what the total nightly charge will be.

ENQUIRY TYPE 02

Double occupancy

If two people are travelling together, confirm the bed arrangement, maximum occupancy, bathroom arrangements, included amenities and any additional guest charges.

ENQUIRY TYPE 03

Twin-share accommodation

Ask whether separate beds can be provided, whether the room is suitable for colleagues or friends, and whether the quoted price covers both occupants.

ENQUIRY TYPE 04

Family or group stay

For families or groups, ask about room capacity, adjoining rooms, extra bedding, children’s arrangements and whether a group booking can be accommodated.

ENQUIRY TYPE 05

Longer stays

For work assignments or extended visits, ask about weekly or monthly arrangements, housekeeping frequency, laundry options, payment schedule and any long-stay conditions.

ENQUIRY TYPE 06

Special requirements

Discuss accessibility needs, ground-floor access, mobility considerations, quiet-room requests or other requirements before confirming a reservation.

BOOKING CHECKLIST

Information to have ready when you enquire.

Providing complete details helps the team respond to your accommodation request. Before contacting the Inn, prepare the following information.

Travel dates

Give your intended arrival date and time, departure date and the number of nights you plan to stay.

Number of guests

State the number of adults and children, and explain whether separate beds or family arrangements are required.

Room preferences

Describe your preferred bed arrangement, bathroom preference and any essential room facilities.

Arrival arrangements

Tell the Inn your expected arrival time and ask how to check in, especially if arriving late or outside normal reception hours.

Payment confirmation

Ask about the deposit, accepted payment methods, balance due date, receipt and cancellation conditions before paying.

Special requests

Raise accessibility, dietary, mobility, parking or other needs early so the property can advise whether it can assist.

GUEST INFORMATION

Questions to ask before check-in.

What time is check-in?

Confirm the official check-in and check-out times and whether early arrival or late departure can be arranged.

What identification is needed?

Ask what identification or booking details guests should present at reception.

Are meals included?

Confirm whether breakfast or any other meal is included in the room rate, or whether café purchases are separate.

What are the guest rules?

Ask about visitors, noise, smoking, alcohol, children, pets if relevant, and any other house rules.

How do I get help?

Confirm the reception or after-hours contact process for urgent assistance, maintenance and security concerns.

What if plans change?

Request the cancellation, amendment, refund and no-show terms that apply to your specific booking.
