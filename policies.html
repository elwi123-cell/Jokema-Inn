IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Before you book, visit or use a service.

Public business-directory information identifies Jokema’s Inn at Portion 764C, Butibam Road, Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea, and lists the telephone number 7052 3210. The public listing does not publish a complete set of accommodation, payment, cancellation or visitor terms. The guidance below is therefore presented as practical information and reasonable expectations—not as confirmed fees, deadlines or binding Jokema Inn rules.

Please obtain confirmation from management.

Before paying or travelling, ask for the current price, service details, reservation status, payment instructions and any conditions that apply to your particular booking. An enquiry alone does not guarantee a room, event space or other service.

01 / Reservations

Booking enquiries and confirmation

Provide accurate dates, guest numbers, contact details and the service you require. Ask management to confirm availability, the agreed price, what is included and whether a deposit or advance payment is required. Keep written confirmation and any receipt.

02 / Changes and cancellations

Amendments, cancellations and no-shows

Contact the venue as early as possible if you need to change or cancel an arrangement. Ask management to explain any applicable notice period, cancellation charge, refund conditions or non-arrival consequences before you confirm. No specific cancellation deadline or fee is published here.

03 / Payments

Prices, deposits and receipts

Confirm the total amount and accepted payment methods directly with authorised management. Do not rely on payment details received from an unverified source. Check the recipient name and amount before transferring funds, and request a receipt or written acknowledgement.

04 / Arrival and departure

Check-in, check-out and service times

Ask management to confirm arrival and departure times for your reservation. If you expect to arrive late, leave later than planned or need a special arrangement, notify the venue in advance. Do not assume that early check-in or late check-out is available or free.

05 / Guest conduct

Respectful behaviour

Guests and visitors are expected to treat staff, other customers, neighbours and property respectfully. Follow reasonable instructions from venue staff, avoid threatening or disruptive behaviour, and respect the rights and comfort of other people using the premises.

06 / Safety and security

Personal safety and property

Take reasonable care of your belongings and personal safety. Keep valuables secure, supervise children in your care and report hazards, damage or concerning incidents to staff promptly. Follow posted safety notices and lawful directions from authorised personnel.

07 / Food and beverages

Café and bar services

Menus, prices, availability and service hours may change. Ask staff about ingredients and dietary requirements before ordering. Alcohol service, where offered, is subject to applicable Papua New Guinea laws and venue requirements. Staff may refuse service where required by law or for safety.

08 / Events and groups

Functions and group arrangements

Discuss event dates, attendance, duration, services, equipment, setup and payment arrangements with management in advance. Ask for the agreed terms in writing, including any deposit, cancellation conditions, permitted activities and responsibility for damage or additional costs.

09 / Vehicles and car wash

Vehicle service enquiries

Confirm the car-wash service, price, expected waiting time and vehicle-handling arrangements before handing over your vehicle. Remove or secure valuables and disclose any existing damage or special handling requirements. Ask staff how keys and vehicles are managed while work is being carried out.

10 / Privacy

Personal information

Share only the personal information reasonably needed to make your enquiry or reservation. Use contact channels confirmed by the business. Avoid sending passwords, banking PINs or other confidential security information. Ask management how booking information is collected, used and retained.

PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

What to confirm before you pay.

  • The exact service, room or event arrangement being provided.
  • The dates, arrival time, duration and number of guests or attendees.
  • The full price, currency, inclusions and any additional charges.
  • Whether a deposit or advance payment is required and the verified payment method.
  • The cancellation, amendment, refund and non-arrival conditions that apply.
  • The name or role of the person confirming the arrangement and how to contact the venue.
  • A written confirmation and payment receipt, where applicable.
ISSUES AND FEEDBACK

Questions, concerns or complaints.

If you have a concern about a booking or service, contact management as soon as reasonably possible. Explain the issue clearly and include the date, service involved and any booking or receipt reference. Keep copies of relevant messages and payment records. For an urgent safety incident, alert venue staff and contact the appropriate emergency services.

These information pages do not limit any rights or protections available to customers under applicable Papua New Guinea law.
